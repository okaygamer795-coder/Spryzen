  idevice_id.exe, ideviceinstaller.exe
    Windows builds of libimobiledevice (device detection by UDID + installing IPAs).

  zsign.exe
    Windows build of zsign (resigns the IPA with your free certificate).

and tells you exactly which tool is missing when you press a button.
